# Retained gameplay HUD coverage

With `ui_retained 1`, screen-space gameplay HUDs submit through the retained
RmlUi renderer. This extends the independent ammo/health/armor status bar to
the remaining HUD layers. `coverage.json` is the audited inventory;
`artwork.rui` contains 265 editable vector symbols and panel constructions,
with 977 material aliases. Both are generated by `tools/ui/build_retained_hud.py`.
`hud_symbols.json` holds the first-party atlas drawings. No retail pixels are
included in these sources.

## Covered presentation routes

| Family | Elements |
|---|---|
| Marine and Strogg | Crosshairs and interaction brackets, weapon strip, pickup notices, powerups, radio, damage direction, boss health/shield, flatbed/tram indicators, save and exit notices |
| Multiplayer | Team/match scores, timer, flag state, awards, frag/round/vote/spectator notices, teammate information, chat and voice panels, scoreboard and tournament brackets, end-match summary |
| Stock vehicles and cinematics | Walker/tank gauges and weapons, air-defence scope, medical activation, body-table readouts/static, cinematic framing and title imagery |
| Awakening | Marine/Strogg/cannon HUDs, ship status and warning darts, speeder progress/timers/boost, GEV gauges, MCC cannon and hull readout, dropship readout and introduction captions |
| Game-owned overlays | Hit markers in SP/MP, MP floating damage numbers, movement-input display and the client network graph |

The registry lists 17 retail roots and 10 expansion roots. Expansion-only
definitions still come from the user's installed `q4xbase` content. They do
not become active in retail or multiplayer. The existing scopes, weapon
wheels and view effects keep their dedicated retained documents.

## State and rendering ownership

The legacy GUI continues to evaluate its existing state dictionary, named
events, visibility, localized strings, animations, input and save data.
`idDeviceContext` records its evaluated draw commands after clipping and
transformation, instead of drawing those commands immediately. `idRetainedHud`
resolves decorative materials to the canonical vector catalog, and
`HudElement` prepares the complete presentation before submitting it through
the retained renderer. It preserves mirrored/cropped UVs and clips vectors
against the exact transformed quadrilateral. Font atlas quads preserve the
original font, layout, inline colors and caret. Complex objective photos,
body diagrams, maps and title imagery remain installed-content images.

Game-owned overlays publish equivalent packets through
`idUserInterfaceManager::DrawHudPrimitives`. Their gameplay calculations,
damage information policy and visibility checks stay at their existing call
sites. The optional movement display only reads the existing user command;
the UI adapter does not request or synthesize input.

Colored item material variants retain their intrinsic colors. Additive implant
cells and plate glows remain unlit when their animated RGB reaches black.
The radio/voice adapters use playback history, including the expansion's
radio panels. This does not implement the currently missing MP voice backend.

The status bar suppresses its original six gauge containers only after its
own retained draw succeeds. The general HUD bridge captures the other
containers. Therefore no old gauge is added behind a successful new gauge.
The status-bar family selection also recognizes Awakening's two on-foot HUDs.

## Failure and lifetime

`ui_retained 0` uses the original presentation. A mod that owns a stock-named
GUI keeps ownership. An invalid registry/catalog, unknown material, missing
image or geometry preparation failure rejects the entire captured GUI frame
and draws its stock presentation in that frame. No partially prepared retained
geometry is published. Failure diagnostics are emitted once per distinct
reason for the GUI instance. Legacy script clocks prevent fallback from
executing timed actions twice.

HUD views participate in the existing managed renderer/language resource
reload. Geometry is cached until its shape, UVs, viewport or tint changes.
Generated overlay views are bounded and cleared at map load/shutdown; GUI
views follow their owning GUI lifetime. Objective image aliases are registered
during the deferred level image load.

`HUD_COVERAGE` records the first nonempty successful frame per instance.
`ui_hudTest <source.gui> report` reports the current draw receipt, including
empty/hidden GUI frames. Its `state`, `event` and `presentation` operations
are developer diagnostics using semantic GUI APIs, without device input.
The MP cheat command `testHudOverlays` stages transient combat indicators.

## Validation

`openq4-ui-hud` exercises every catalog symbol through the native retained
runtime, exact sheared clipping, tint/cache reuse, mirrored UVs, glyph atlas
UVs, portrait geometry, missing art/image rejection, hidden frames and
document/renderer reload. `openq4-ui-hud-coverage` checks generator freshness,
catalog references and agreement between the audited roots and runtime routes.
Its optional `--assets` and `--awakening` arguments also inspect supplied
GUI definitions, including expansion overrides of retail names, and dynamic
item/weapon definitions for unmapped materials or unregistered HUD roots.

`tools/ui/capture_hud_coverage.py` loads actual gameplay before exercising
installed HUD definitions, including normally hidden named-event states.
It uses windowed hidden clients, isolated writable profiles, disabled host
mouse/controller input and the engine's `screenshot` command. The retained
adapter mutation suite and existing status-bar native checks remain required.
Local reports and engine captures are under `.tmp/hud-coverage/`.

`tools/ui/capture_hud_network.py` starts the staged dedicated server and a
loopback client. It requires active multiplayer gameplay and an actual retained
network-graph submission, then switches Stock UI off/on and shuts both processes
down. `ui_manager_lifecycle.py` also verifies retirement of the generated HUD
views at map load and before retained renderer shutdown.

### 7 October 2026 validation

The client, dedicated server and both game modules build. The staged binaries
match their `builddir` counterparts. All three registered Meson suites pass:
1,269,626 HUD checks, 2,422,820 status-bar checks and the catalog/route audit.
The installed-content audit covers 33 GUI definitions across the 27 roots,
including Awakening overrides and dynamic weapon/item icons. The adapter's
production case and all 17 mutation checks pass; manager lifetime and retained
gate checks also pass.

Ten engine profiles preserve 109 lossless captures: retail families on OpenGL
and Vulkan, portrait retail families at 720x1280, all ten Awakening roots,
Marine/Strogg and multiplayer gameplay, a Vulkan client connected to the
dedicated server, save/load and `vid_restart`, active stock fallback after a
broken material registry, and real decoded radio playback. Normal profiles
have no HUD fallback or retained-renderer warnings; the fallback profile
requires its expected diagnostic and a failed retained draw receipt.

The evidence index is `.tmp/hud-coverage/evidence/validation.json`; its sibling
`final-sha256.json` records the final staged binaries and sources. Each profile
keeps its engine log, original launch report, script and PNGs. Original reports
retain their launch-time paths; the index points to the preserved files.

The initial Vulkan/portrait fixture named a nonexistent stock crosshair; the
corrected OpenGL family profile uses the installed blaster crosshair and has
no such warning. The radio fixture waits for rendered frames after switching
UI modes before starting a timed sample, so cold resource work cannot consume
its wall-clock wait before the audio world updates. The final radio run passes
all ten playback/capture checks.

Unrelated findings remain: multiplayer's q4dm1 content requests absent AAS
sizes, reports an item in solid, and has an effect material with an empty
stage. The supplied Awakening alpha content reports missing old textures,
model-order and map/AI warnings; its vector HUD submissions still succeed.
The unused retail `vehicle_phatride` test definition names a missing
`guis/vehicles/turret_hud.gui`; no supplied HUD exists to migrate for that entity.
The live MP microphone/codec backend remains unimplemented. These checks do
not claim those content or audio-backend issues are resolved.

Automatic approval review blocked removal of the disposable profiles under
`.tmp/hud-coverage/`. They remain alongside the curated `evidence/` directory.

## Scope of this increment

This closes presentation routing for the listed screen-space HUD families.
It retains GUI script evaluation and existing layouts. It does not complete
all Remastered behavior and layout enhancements in the visual specification,
the full menu/editor migration, or the world-surface GUI migration. On-model
weapon/cockpit screens and interactive world monitors still use their surface
renderer. Cross-platform and full campaign encounter acceptance remain in the
[UI plan](../archive/2026-10-18/plans/idtech5-ui.md). The network graph preserves its client-only
gate and is qualified with a connected client, rather than a listen-server view.

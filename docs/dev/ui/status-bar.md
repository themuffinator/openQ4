# Retained gameplay status bar

This is the historical standalone status-bar checkpoint. The active on-foot
implementation is now part of the [complete Remastered HUD](remastered-hud.md),
generated by `build_remastered_hud.py` and presented by `idRemasteredHud`.
The old `status_bar_*.rui` documents are not the gameplay route; their earlier
validation below does not qualify the current HUD.

With `ui_retained 1`, the normal on-foot HUD draws the new ammo, health and
armor gauges through retained vector documents. Both game modules include
the same player implementation. Marine and multiplayer use the Marine
document; the post-stroggification campaign uses the Strogg document selected
by its actual HUD source.

## Presentation

- The three gauges keep their stock spacing as a centered group at the bottom
  of the viewport. The Marine group is 438 source units wide; the Strogg
  group's larger stock plates occupy 449 units. Armor disappearing never
  moves the ammunition or health gauges. Wide and portrait views use isotropic
  scaling and a 4:3 center-screen reference.
- Editable vectors reconstruct the backing, notched fill, armor cells, EKG,
  warning rim, item symbols and weapon silhouettes. The original family fonts
  occupy fixed digit positions. Marine art and digits retain their shear;
  Strogg art has the opposite shear with upright digits.
- SP weapons with clips of at least two show clip and reserve. Clipless and
  single-shot weapons show total ammo against their maximum. MP always shows
  total ammo in the weapon color. Unlimited weapons leave both numbers blank
  with a full gauge. Inventory maxima clamp fills without capping the displayed
  overhealth/armor value.
- Gains brighten/enlarge the digits over 250 ms; damage does not. Low health
  changes the plate color and pulses its rim at 2 Hz. Reduced motion disables
  the gain pop while retaining the EKG instrument motion.
- `ui_hudScale` (0.5–2, default 1) is independent of menu scale.
  `ui_hudSafeInset` (0–10 percent, default 0) moves the bar inward. Narrow views
  fit the whole group without modifying either archived preference.

## Ownership and fallback

`DrawHudWithStatusBar` runs at the existing main-HUD draw site, after gameplay
visibility checks. Vehicle, objective, camera, spectator and scoreboard
ownership remain with the game. Each gauge also observes its legacy container's
effective visibility, including script-driven changes.
The single-player weapon silhouette also follows the legacy weapon-image
visibility; multiplayer keeps it hidden.

The new document renders first and exposes a read-only
`__retained::drawSucceeded` presentation receipt. Only a successful draw
suppresses the six legacy gauge containers during the following legacy HUD
draw. The suppression state is then removed. It never overrides expressions,
changes script state, or enters save serialization. Radio, notices, boss bars,
weapon strip and other HUD components continue using their existing owners.

Missing/invalid documents and mod-owned HUDs keep the stock gauges. Gate changes
retry loading. Renderer resource recovery keeps the managed retained instance;
failed draws leave the stock gauges visible in the same frame. Presentation
instances are unique, transient and freed on player destruction/restoration.
Existing saves keep their GUI framing unchanged.

## Sources and validation

`tools/ui/build_retained_status_bar.py` generates both documents from the
project's first-party atlas geometry and the tracked `status_bar_icons.json`.
No extracted proprietary textures or third-party code are added.

`UiStatusBarChecks.inl` runs through the real document compiler and retained
runtime. `tools/ui/capture_status_bar.py` qualifies SP/MP gameplay using semantic
developer commands and engine-render-target screenshots, with a hidden window
and host mouse/controller input disabled. Task evidence is kept under
`.tmp/status-bar/evidence/` (capture index: `validation.json`; build/source
identity: `final-sha256.json`). Disposable profiles, saves and TGA duplicates
are separate from the retained logs and lossless PNG captures.

Validation on Windows x64 covers Marine campaign gameplay on OpenGL, Strogg
campaign and multiplayer gameplay on Vulkan, and a 720x1280 portrait campaign
view. Engine captures cover ammo consumption, low health and zero armor,
recovery, scaling/inset, reduced motion, Stock UI switching, unlimited and
clipless weapons, HUD hiding, renderer restart and campaign save/load.
Separate private-mod profiles verify missing/invalid documents and mod-owned
HUD fallback. Native layout checks include 4K and wide/portrait viewports;
the retained adapter checks successful and failed draw receipts. The broader
retained-screen suite passes as well.

The gameplay logs also contain unrelated existing map/AAS and Vulkan shadow
warnings. They are retained with the capture evidence; these checks do not
claim those engine/content issues are resolved.

The subsequent [HUD coverage increment](hud-coverage.md) adds retained
presentation for the remaining screen-space HUD components and vehicle displays.
The editor, full Remastered layouts and wider UI programme remain separate gates.

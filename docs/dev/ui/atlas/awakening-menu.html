The Awakening · in-game menus

Visual specification 1.45, sections 2, 4, 6, 8 and 13.7. While The Awakening is the running campaign, the single-player pause menu and everything it opens become one design drawn from two sources: the Valkaryne, the campaign's final enemy, and the cold of the cryo facility that leads to her lair, drawn as the frozen 0.14.1 Awakening Edition release artwork draws it. Her black armour is the frame, her blades shape its ends and horns, and her reactor lens lights the control you are on in the one warm color. Her eyes are her only cold light, and that cold is the color of everything else. Every level of the menu moves as one: the frame glides, the levels scroll beneath it, and the rime catches the light as the edges travel.

Frame construction

One curve shapes every frame: the sweep, a 30° arc that leaves an edge at 45° and arrives at 15°, so a sweep c high is 1.73 c long on a radius of 3.86 c, and it always hollows the frame it cuts. A blade is a plate's open end cut by one sweep down to its rail. A horn is a step of 24 u or more: the edge runs on to a point and one sweep cuts back behind it. Every smaller change of level is a straight 30° flank, both sides of a pocket alike, so the bands read as one even rhythm with the horns as their only accents. The lens is the only round thing: two rings and a halo around the focused marker.

Band motion

The glide is one curve, cubic (0.65, 0, 0.35, 1): every move starts and settles without a jolt, where the Marine family's accel(a, d) switches its acceleration on and off. Going to a page the bottom band leads and the top band follows 40 ms later on a shorter move, so the two arrive together. The rime line rises from 0.45 with a band's speed to 0.90 at 1 u per ms, so light runs along the edges only while they travel.

Frost construction

One crystal, enlarged. It leaves a seed on the view's top or bottom edge in 3 u steps, wandering 6.9° a step and thinning 3% a step from 0.75–1.6 u to a 0.5 u floor. At each step it has a 28% chance of a branch at 60° ± 8.6° to either side, 25–50% as long and 70% as wide, to four levels. Every angle in the family is a multiple of 15°: 45° cuts, 30° flanks, 15° points, 60° ice.

Menu levels and the scroll between them

Four levels, as the Marine menus have them: the pause, a page (Save, Load, Restart Level, Objectives, Settings), a sub-page (a settings category) and a detail page. The bottom band docks once, at the first page; the top band docks with it and then moves one notch pitch, 97 u, toward the trailing edge for each level deeper, so its 30° flank stands between the crumbs as the separator in the path.

Going deeper everything travels toward the trailing edge: the level you leave scrolls 640 u out on the glide while it fades over its first half, the label you chose is carried into the title slot, the old title rises into the band as the crumb, and the arriving level scrolls in the last 32 u from the leading side, settling with page.enter's ease-out over 250 ms as it fades in over 150 ms. It starts as the departing level clears, at 500 ms from the pause and at 300 ms between pages. Back mirrors it toward the leading edge: the title leaves at once, the level scrolls out over 300 ms, the frame glides back from the first frame, and the parent scrolls in from 32 u on the trailing side, its crumb returning to the title slot over the last 150 ms.

Within a level the frame stands still. Tabs scroll their rows sideways in the order the strip reads (page.enter, page.leave), and a list that runs past its edge scrolls by whole rows on the glide over 150 ms, never by a jump. Input during any of this retargets it from where it is.

Where the design comes from

From the Valkaryne Her armour is black: the bands, plates and dialog body. Every limb ends in a blade that sweeps to a point: the plates' and buttons' ends, the plinth's end and the needle rules. Her legs and scythes hook outward: the horns of the bands' large steps, one on the top band reaching for the level block and two spread under the raised section as she stands, with the horned corners of the level block and the dialog. Her five orange reactor lenses (#FF8E00 in her glow map) are the lens around the focused marker, in the interaction orange. She moves without a jolt; so does the frame. Her eyes are cyan, the one cold light on her, and so is the menu's light.

From the artwork The glacial cyan of the plate's rim and the version figure (#78DEFF) for rails and values, the white of its lettering (#EEF8FF), the deep ice of its plate body (#040E1A), the rim-and-glow edge as the rime line over the bands' rim light, the branching frost, the two depths of snow and the low haze.

Left out A sweep that bulges out of a frame or rounds a horn's throat, which reads as a knuckle; rows of small sweeps, which read as scallops; mixed angles on one pocket, which read as a saw. Her crown of spikes and any picture of her. The artwork's 13° slant on furniture, its frosted-glass lift, grain and flare, and icicles.

Where frost may grow Only on the framing bands, which are opaque black and hold no content: it fades out across the last 6 u inside a band's inner edge, horns included, and never crosses it. Frost belongs to the view, not the band, so a moving band slides beneath it and uncovers or covers it. Each level carves its own band text out of it, with 6 u clear and a 6 u feather: the prompt bar, the titles and crumbs.

Opening The menu fades in over 250 ms as the stock does while the bands close in from 30 u beyond their places over 400 ms, easing out; the crystal front leaves every seed at one speed and the longest crystal finishes at 900 ms. Snow falls behind the instruments along the artwork's 13° line, far flakes at 6 u/s and near ones at 14 u/s. The lens appears at once with focus, as the stock highlight does. Reduced motion places bands and levels at once, shows the frost complete and the snow still; high contrast drops the snow, haze and frost, and the Phone profile's power saving drops the snow.

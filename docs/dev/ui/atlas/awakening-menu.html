The Awakening · in-game menus

Visual specification 1.41, sections 2, 4, 6, 8 and 13.7. While The Awakening is the running campaign, the single-player pause menu and everything it opens become one design drawn from two sources: the Valkaryne, the campaign's final enemy, and the cold of the cryo facility that leads to her lair, drawn as the frozen 0.14.1 Awakening Edition release artwork draws it. Her black armour is the frame, her blades shape its ends and steps, and her reactor lens lights the control you are on in the one warm color. Her eyes are her only cold light, and that cold is the color of everything else. The layout, type, pitch and motion are the Marine family's, unchanged.

Frame construction

One curve shapes every frame: the sweep, a 30° arc that leaves an edge at 45° and arrives at 15°, so a sweep c high is 1.73 c long on a radius of 3.86 c. A blade is a plate's open end cut by one sweep down to its rail. A talon stands at the foot of every 45° step of 24 u or more: a prong a third as high as the step, along the thicker side's edge, cut back to the step by one sweep. The lens is the only round thing: two rings and a halo around the focused marker.

Frost construction

One crystal, enlarged. It leaves a seed on the view's top or bottom edge in 3 u steps, wandering 6.9° a step and thinning 3% a step from 0.75–1.6 u to a 0.5 u floor. At each step it has a 28% chance of a branch at 60° ± 8.6° to either side, 25–50% as long and 70% as wide, to four levels. Every angle in the family is a multiple of 15°: 45° cuts, 15° points, 60° ice.

Where the design comes from

From the Valkaryne Her armour is black: the bands, plates and dialog body. Every limb ends in a blade that sweeps to a point: the plates' and buttons' ends, the plinth's end and the needle rules. Her legs and scythes hook outward: the talons on the bands' steps, two spread under the raised section as she stands, and one at the dialog's foot. Five orange reactor lenses (#FF8E00 in her glow map) are the lens around the focused marker, in the interaction orange. Her eyes are cyan, the one cold light on her, and so is the menu's light.

From the artwork The glacial cyan of the plate's rim and the version figure (#78DEFF) for rails and values, the white of its lettering (#EEF8FF), the deep ice of its plate body (#040E1A), the rim-and-glow edge as the rime line over the bands' rim light, the branching frost, the two depths of snow and the low haze.

Left out A sweep on a closed panel's corner cut, which reads as a rounded corner: cards and the dialog keep straight 45° cuts. Rows of small sweeps on the bands, which read as scallops. Her crown of spikes and any picture of her. The artwork's 13° slant on furniture, its frosted-glass lift, grain and flare, and icicles.

Where frost may grow Only on the framing bands, which are opaque black and hold no content: it fades out across the last 6 u inside a band's inner edge, talons included, and never crosses it. Each screen carves its own band text out of it, with 6 u clear and a 6 u feather: here the prompt bar. A notice brings a black backing of the same margin.

Motion The menu fades in over 250 ms as the stock does; from that first frame the crystal front leaves every seed at one speed and the longest crystal finishes at 900 ms, easing out. Snow falls behind the instruments along the artwork's 13° line, far flakes at 6 u/s and near ones at 14 u/s. The lens appears at once with focus, as the stock highlight does. Reduced motion shows the frost complete and the snow still; high contrast drops the snow, haze and frost, and the Phone profile's power saving drops the snow.

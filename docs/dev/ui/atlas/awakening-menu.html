The Awakening · in-game menus

Visual specification 1.40, sections 2, 4, 6, 8 and 13.7. While The Awakening is the running campaign, the single-player pause menu and everything it opens trade the Marine family's olive light for glacial ice, after the frozen 0.14.1 Awakening Edition release artwork. The frame, the 45° cuts, the open plates and the ◥ marker are the Marine family's, unchanged; the color of the light, the frost on the bands and the snow behind the instruments are the theme. Orange stays the one warm color: it still means the control you are on.

Frost construction

One crystal, enlarged. It leaves a seed on the view's top or bottom edge in 3 u steps, wandering 6.9° a step and thinning 3% a step from 0.75–1.6 u to a 0.5 u floor. At each step it has a 28% chance of a branch at 60° ± 8.6° to either side, 25–50% as long and 70% as wide, to four levels. Sixty degrees is how ice grows; the furniture's own cuts stay at 45°.

From the artwork to the menu

Taken the glacial cyan of the plate's rim and the version figure (#78DEFF) for rails and values, the white of its lettering (#EEF8FF), the deep ice of the plate's body (#040E1A), the rim-and-glow edge as the bands' rime line over their rim light, the branching frost from the edges and corners, the two depths of snow and the haze in the lower part of the picture.

Left the 13° slant of the plate and tagline, which belongs to the wordmark: furniture keeps its 45° cuts and no shear, and the slant survives only as the snow's fall line. The frosted-glass lift and rime grain, which would grey the scene behind the menu. The lens flare on the wordmark's slab. Icicles, removed from the artwork itself.

Where frost may grow only on the framing bands, which are opaque black and hold no content: it fades out across the last 6 u inside a band's inner edge and never crosses it. Each screen carves its own band text out of it, with 6 u clear and a 6 u feather: here the prompt bar. A notice brings a black backing of the same margin. Frost never lies on a plate, a card, a dialog or text.

Motion the menu fades in over 250 ms as the stock does; from that first frame the crystal front leaves every seed at one speed and the longest crystal finishes at 900 ms, easing out. Snow falls behind the instruments along the artwork's 13° line, far flakes at 6 u/s and near ones at 14 u/s. Reduced motion shows the frost complete and the snow still; high contrast and the Phone profile's power saving drop the snow, and high contrast drops the haze and frost too.

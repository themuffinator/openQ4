Atlas - multiplayer HUD

Sections 14.9 and 14.14. Remastered rebuilds the top of the screen from compact HUD plates instead of the stock's full-height fading slabs, so nothing darkens the view beyond its own text. The standing or team panels, timer and round clock sit top-leading; notices stack in one center column; the kill feed and match context sit top-trailing; chat and its input sit on the leading side; the status bar stays centered. Every cue that the stock gave by color alone also has a shape, tag or marker. Player names are examples. Classic, in the layout controls, shows the stock construction.

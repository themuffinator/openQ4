White ink takes the crosshair color; the black outline and shadow ink stays black at every tint, as in the stock. Pick any crosshair below to preview it.

Color · the stock menu's eight swatches

Machinegun · crosshair_machinegun

The still picture (the stock interface, or the animated crosshair turned off): on a hit the crosshair grows 10 u and returns over 150 ms, tinted red for 100 ms in single player. Hit markers (hud_hitMarker, off by default in both modes) put four marks on the diagonals from half the crosshair size plus 4 dp, so they never cover a design such as the lightning gun's darts. A color change applies at once.

Animated crosshair · on by default in the new interface

hud_crosshairAnimated 1, under ui_retained 1 only: the weapon's own design is drawn live in its pieces and the cursor GUI's picture goes transparent; context cursors, The Awakening's lock-on reticule and any design it does not know keep the still picture. Assemble with the weapon's raise over 260 ms (easing out, parts seating with a 10% overshoot); a weapon change folds the old design away in 70 ms first, and a dot holds the aim point while no design is up. Kick a damped spring (30 rad/s, damping 0.52) struck by every shot, scaled per design. Cycle a weapon that waits 450 ms or more between shots shows the wait: the railgun's bars and the lightning darts draw back home, the rocket halo sweeps round, the grenade quarters stand apart, and a flash marks ready. Charge the blaster's arcs wind shut into a ring that breathes at full charge; letting go is the shot. Spin the hyperblaster's arcs and the gauntlet's three teeth turn; the nailgun's brackets close with the drum and tremble at full rate. Reload and empty the design dims by 45%; a clip of up to twelve rounds shows a pip per round lit as it goes in, anything else a turning sweep; an empty machinegun or rocket launcher turns its ticks onto the diagonals. Lock the nailgun's brackets clamp shut in the stock guide's amber then red with four pips; a vehicle gun closes four corners. Hits squeeze the design and tint it toward the hit color (0.9 of the tier's alpha, 0.45 under reduced flashing; 0.3 of that when hit markers are on, which then dock in the design's clear space); a kill sends one ring out from the design's edge. Hit #F22B24, kill #FFE0D1, team #FFB833, own splash #8C9EB8; tiers last 180, 240, 320 and 420 ms. Reduced motion fades the design in place and stills what turns and flickers. Fire rates and clips are the stock weapon definitions; the vehicle cannon's rate here is illustrative.

Weapon crosshairs

Custom set · the stock cycle order

Twenty entries in the stock order: the eight weapon designs, ten simple marks, the gauntlet and the napalm launcher. Entries 10 and 16 are the same art in the stock, so the picker offers nineteen designs. The picker steps both ways. Vehicles always use their weapon's crosshair.

Context cursors · click to play their stock motion

Context cursors keep their own colors and motion: the use hand pulses 24–28 u every 600 ms; the talk, medic, tech and locked cursors close from 34 to 22 u while fading in over 200 ms; the enter-vehicle chevron rises 7 u and fades over 500 ms above a triangle dimming to 0.40; the friendly X closes from 40 to 34 u. The world-GUI cursor is never drawn while a world GUI has focus, and the friendly X needs the far character-info option off, so both are rare in stock play.

The Awakening · the space cannon's reticule, its guides and two new cursors

The space cannon's lock-on reticule changes art and color with the lock: white while nothing is targeted, yellow while its rockets acquire, #40FF40 once locked, #FF1A1A over a friendly target and white again, without the side bars, out of range. Its pulse cannon shows only the white, out-of-range and friendly states. Three guide effects turn 0.3 turn a second around targets: the stock nailgun lock ring in amber while the rockets acquire, the lock guide in red once locked, and the stock ring in gray around the nearest enemy. Gray brackets frame a target out of range, but the stock material adds nothing, so only Remastered shows them. The busy-talk cursor stays red. The speeder's cannon and the stock walker's cannon name a vehicle_cannon crosshair that neither game ships, so openQ4 draws one. The expansion's new weapons borrow stock crosshairs: the Core Cannon the grenade launcher's, the napalm launcher the rocket launcher's, the Freeze Gun the blaster's, and the grapple the rocket launcher's in yellow.

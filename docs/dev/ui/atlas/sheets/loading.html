Atlas - loading screens

Section 14.17. The loading screen keeps the stock identity: a full-bleed levelshot between two heavy bands, the level name trailing at the top, the dot matrix, the corner brackets and the orange progress bar. Remastered pins every part to the view edge it belongs to, scrolls the frame in over the levelshot, and fills the bands with what a player waits for: the objectives, the loader's phase and count, tips, and a continue prompt for the active device. Multiplayer shows the server card and the map's arsenal as each item spawns, then hands over to the welcome menu. Levelshots here are vector stand-ins; in the game they are the bitmap exception.

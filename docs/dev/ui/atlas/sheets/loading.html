Atlas - loading screens

Section 14.17. The loading screen keeps the stock identity: a full-bleed levelshot between two heavy bands, the level name trailing at the top, the dot matrix, the corner brackets and the orange progress bar. Remastered carries the bands out to the edges of any view and fills them with what a player waits for: the mission and its objectives, a progress readout that names its current stage, tips, and a continue prompt in the active device's glyphs. Multiplayer shows the server card and the map's arsenal as each item spawns, then hands over to the welcome menu. Levelshots here are vector stand-ins; in the game they are the bitmap exception.

MultiplayerStock raises the bottom band to 227 u for the server lines and a row of 24 u item icons. Remastered keeps the picture tall: the server card sits on the leading side, the arsenal fills in along the band as each item spawns, and the screen gives way to the welcome menu instead of a continue prompt.

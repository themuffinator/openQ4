Atlas - multiplayer menus

Section 14.18. In a match the menu never takes the whole screen. One card holds a horizontal tab strip and sits in the center screen over the live view, which the game softens; the match keeps running around it and nothing pretends to pause the server. Welcome greets a player who has just connected with the match, the join choices and the server's rules. Escape is the in-match menu: Resume leads, and team, players, vote, Match Control, settings, voice, server and admin each have a tab. Switch tabs with Q and E, the bumpers, the arrow keys or a click; Esc leaves. Player names are examples.

PlacementA card in the center screen, never the whole view: 380×268 u for Welcome and 432×318 u for Escape, so at 16:9 the live view stays visible on every side. The card uses the section 6 construction: a 0.94 black body inside one rail, 12 dp and 3 dp cuts, a 40 dp header with its wash.
TabsThe stock tab strip, horizontal: the active tab rises 20 u with a small leading flare and a 12 u 45° trailing shoulder, filled #5A652A from 1.00 to 0.60, the baseline breaking under it and a wash fading below. Widths fit the labels. The bumper glyphs sit at the strip's ends; focus is an orange rule, never the selection's fill.
WelcomeJoin, Server, Players and Settings. Join leads with the match and the choices the game mode allows: the two team cards and Auto join, or Join game, or the Tourney queue, and Spectate. Esc spectates for now; Leave server sits at the trailing end of the prompt bar.
EscapeTeam, Players, Vote, Match, Settings, Voice, Server and Admin. Resume leads the prompt bar; Main menu and Disconnect trail it, and Disconnect asks first in the stock modal. Match Control keeps its own six sections as a second, smaller strip.
Unavailable actionsStay in place, dimmed, with a lock and the reason on the plate: a full team, a vote already running, a captain-only pause. Choosing one flashes the reason instead of doing nothing.
Soft focusThe view behind is softened by the scene effect, not dimmed: it ramps in over 250 ms as the card rises 8 u and fades in over 150 ms, and releases on every way out. Where softening is unavailable the fallback is a darkening scrim and vignette.

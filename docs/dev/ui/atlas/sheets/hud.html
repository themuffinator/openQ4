Atlas - HUD

Section 14. The HUD is its own family: rounded plates, sheared gauges and bevelled fills, never the menu's 45° plates. The Remastered layout keeps the 4:3 stock canvas as the center screen and expands outward: a wider view adds a leading and a trailing screen, and a narrower one adds top and bottom bands. Each group keeps its stock offset from the edge it belongs to and moves out with that edge. The status bar stays in the center screen. Change the aspect ratio, HUD scale or Classic layout below, and show the screen regions to see where each group anchors.

Center screenThe 4:3 stock canvas at full height, or at full width in a view narrower than 4:3: the crosshair, hit indicator, status bar, pickup lines, weapon strip, boss bar and centered notices.
Leading and trailing screensThe width a wider view adds: 160 dp each at 16:9, 373 dp at 21:9. Edge groups move out into them; at 32:9 they stop at the 21:9 edges unless the HUD width is Full. The transmission display always takes the trailing screen's top corner, on foot and in every vehicle.
Top and bottom bandsThe height a view narrower than 4:3 adds: 16 u each at 5:4 and 329 u (370 dp) each in the 9:16 portrait view. Top and bottom groups move out into them; the center screen stays centered.
ClassicThe stock 4:3 rectangles, centered, with no expansion: the gauges return to 13, 190 and 326 u and the pickup line to the ammo gauge.

Status bar the stock gauges and gaps, centered as one group on the bottom edge · pickup lines up to two, centered over the gauges, newest nearest them, repeats merged with a count; each holds 3000 ms · weapon strip above the pickup lines with its name above the slots; the stock shows it for 1000 ms after a change · radio chatter on the trailing edge · fill anchored at the trailing end, draining toward the numerals over a 20% track · gains pop the numeral to 120% in white over 250 ms · hit indicator a #FF1A1A dome 227 dp from the center, rotated toward the source, 0.70 to zero over 500 ms · low health #FF4C00 at once with an additive rim pulsing at 2 Hz.

Marine gauge · measured anatomy, ×3

Backing 123×40 u, radii 1–1.4 u, a 6.9 u foot under the trailing third with a 45° flank. Fill inset 4.4 u and 4.6 u, bevelled 58% to 97% at every edge. Notch: a ledge at a third of the fill height, 14.2 u long, then a concave arc of radius 16.6 u. The icon sits in the notch.

Strogg gauge · the opposite shear

129×67 u: a raised tab and 30° leading shoulder, a fill stepping down its leading edge and cut away below its trailing third, #FF9000 fills, upright R_Strogg numerals in #FCFFC8, and a masked grain layer. Sheared +0.22, tops toward the leading edge.

Transmission · the measured voice trace, ×4

The trace is waveform.tga rebuilt column by column: a 4-texel core with the recorded speech bursts above and below it, 37% of the columns flat. It scrolls toward the leading edge at 24.6 u/s inside a right trapezoid 19.2 u wide at the top and 23.1 u at the bottom, whose leading side leans with the plate, and adds #FF8000 to the plate. Classic scales its height by the canned table, 0.71 to 2.5 times, a new entry every 79 ms; Remastered draws the actual decoded audio peaks at the playback cursor. Choose a local audio file above and press Play; silence is flat, pausing holds the graph, and seeking moves it with the audio.

EKG · the measured beat, ×3

One beat of ekg.tga, twice per gauge width: a baseline at 58% of the rect, a triangular P wave, a small Q dip, an R spike rising 17.5 u, an S trough 6.2 u deep and triangular T and U waves 6.2 u and 3.0 u high. A 0.95 u core carries a halo falling from 0.24 to nothing over 3.7 u. It is added through the fill's outline in #161B0E (#4C0D00 at low health), beneath the fill, and travels toward the trailing edge at 25 u/s.

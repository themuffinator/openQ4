Atlas - detailing

Construction at four times magnification. Detail decides whether a reconstruction reads as authored or traced: continuous mitered rails, 45° cuts with equal extents, markers inside the cap band, separators that stop short of rails, and numbers set with tabular figures.

Cut and rail join · ×4

A 45° cut keeps equal horizontal and vertical extent after layout; the rail runs through it as one continuous stroke. Grid: one physical pixel at the reference.

Joins · correct and avoided

Separate segments brighten where their antialiased ends overlap and open gaps at fractional scales. Draw each rail as one path.

Cut ladder

Only the measured cuts appear: 3 dp minor, the settings row's 6 dp upper-leading cut, 7.5 dp (5 u) tags and filter buttons, 12 dp cards and DISPLAY dialogs, the modal's 13 dp raised corner, 17 dp section and navigation plates, 20 dp actions and the 38 dp modal chamfer, which wider dialogs stretch up to 56 dp. A cut never falls below 3 dp on an essential control.

Black frame at the title's tab · ×4

The tab starts at the frame's leading edge, carries the 6 dp corner marker 14 dp before the title, and steps down at 45° to the slot's floor 10 dp past the title's end (the Strogg family's 30°). The glow still shows in the slot beyond it and around the 3 dp outline.

Marker in the cap band · ×4

The marker's legs sit inside the cap band of the Marine label it precedes, never on the baseline or the em box.

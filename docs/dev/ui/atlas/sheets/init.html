Atlas - initializing screen

Section 14.17. Before any menu exists, the engine paints one picture and a status line while it starts: LOADING, then each system as it comes up. The stock picture is a single texture. Remastered rebuilds it as separate vector layers (the field, the rings, four clamps, the emblem, the QUAKE 4 lettering and its glow), opens it with an entrance from black, and turns the plate band and the teeth slowly in opposite directions beneath the fixed lettering.

Entrance (Appendix C 1.37, 1.3 s) The first frame, which builds the document, is black, and the black lifts over 240 ms. The mechanism (rings, plates, teeth, clamps and emblem) drops from 1.8× to its seat about the rings' center, decelerating hard, passes 1.4% under size at 820 ms and settles by 1080 ms; the teeth wind in from 150° counterclockwise and the plate band from 90° clockwise, at rest by 960 ms. The clamps wait 16% wide and 20° back, close between 470 and 740 ms and settle by 900 ms; the halo flares 560–760 ms and eases to 0.40 by 1300 ms, where its breathing takes over. The sweep fades in from 600 ms, the status block rises 6 u into place from 640 to 1000 ms, and the glint's first pass crosses the still lettering at 940 ms. Start again replays it; reduced motion shows the screen at rest from the first frame.

Remastered accents. Close to the stock picture, and only in Remastered: a warm light sweeps the rings clockwise once every 10 s; a halo breathes behind the dark emblem; embers rise slowly through the rings; a glint crosses QUAKE 4 every 9 s; and a marker pulses on the status rule. Each new step's line fades in over 280 ms while rising 5 u; the marker glides to its segment over 440 ms and the segments it reaches fill to done over 300 ms, 70 ms apart. Reduced motion keeps the sweep as a key light at the upper left and the halo at 0.80, drops the embers, glint and marker, and changes the line and rule at once.

RingsConcentric about 319,214.5 u, the center of the emblem's own ring, as solid annuli that turn and scale as one mesh: the inner ring at 169–175.5 u, the plate band at 175.5–204.5 u with two framed plates 90° wide and an 8 u frame, the rim at 204.5–210.5 u, eight teeth at 204.5–247.5 u, 22.5° wide on a 45° pitch, and grooves out to 300.5 u. Four clamps sit on the diagonals at 174–211 u. The tones step between #171A09 and #21270C. Classic keeps the stock picture's soft edges and its shade at 3 and 9 o'clock.
Emblem and letteringThe emblem is the traced q4logo in #0E1000, in its 260 u window at 190,125 u (the stock picture centers it on the canvas). QUAKE 4 is the q4text logotype at 0.367 u per texel in #F2F0EC. Its orange halo is rebuilt as vector light: the letters filled and stroked in #FF8340 three times, 12 u wide at 0.016, 8.7 u at 0.027 and 5.3 u at 0.042, all added.
StatusThe stock prints each step in the console's 8×16 u characters, #F09E0D, centered across the canvas with its top at 410 u, and repaints only then. Remastered sets the line in Marine 16 dp with its middle at 412 u, over a rule at 437 u of seven 21 u segments on a 24 u pitch, one per step from SOUND to MENUS: done 0.55, pending 0.15.
MotionOn the presentation clock, the band turns counterclockwise once every 180 s and the teeth clockwise once every 120 s, beneath the fixed clamps, emblem and lettering. The screen repaints up to 60 times a second while loading offers it a moment. Reduced motion holds them still, and Classic never turns them.

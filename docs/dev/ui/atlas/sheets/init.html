Atlas - initializing screen

Section 14.17. Before any menu exists, the engine paints one picture and a status line while it starts: LOADING, then each system as it comes up. The stock picture is a single texture. Remastered rebuilds it as separate vector layers (the field, the rings, two clamps, the emblem, the QUAKE 4 lettering and its glow), so the plate band and the teeth can turn slowly in opposite directions beneath the fixed emblem.

Remastered accents. Close to the stock picture, and only in Remastered: a warm light sweeps the rings clockwise once every 10 s, catching the rims at its leading edge; a halo breathes behind the dark emblem; embers rise slowly through the rings; a glint crosses QUAKE 4 every 9 s; and the active status segment pulses while each new step flickers in. Reduced motion keeps a fixed key light at the upper left and drops the embers, glint and pulse.

RingsConcentric about 319,214.5 u, the center of the emblem's own ring: an inner ring at 169–175 u over a soft edge from 161 u; the plate band at 175–204 u, with two framed plates 90° wide at the top and bottom and an 8 u frame; the rim at 204–210 u, fading into the teeth ring by 222 u; eight teeth at 211–247 u, 22.5° wide on a 45° pitch; grooves out to 300 u. The tones step between #171A09 and #21270C.
Emblem and letteringThe emblem is the traced q4logo in #0E1000, in its 260 u box centered on the canvas. QUAKE 4 is the q4text logotype at 0.367 u per texel in #F2F0EC. Its orange halo is rebuilt as vector light: the letters dilated 5.5 u, blurred with a 2.25 u sigma and added in #FF8340 at 0.10.
StatusThe stock prints each step in the console's 8×16 u characters, #F09E0D, centered across the canvas with its top at 410 u, and repaints only then. Remastered sets the line in Marine 16 dp on the stock line's middle at 418 u and adds a rule under it with one segment per step.
MotionOn the presentation clock, the band turns counterclockwise once every 180 s and the teeth clockwise once every 120 s, beneath the fixed clamps, emblem and lettering. Reduced motion holds them still, and Classic never turns them.

Sequence LOADING... twice, then INITIALIZING SOUND..., NETWORK..., RENDER SYSTEM... and USER INTERFACE..., LOADING GAME... and INITIALIZING MENUS... · the stock stretches the picture over the whole view, and openQ4's aspect correction pillarboxes it in #181A08, as Classic shows · Remastered extends the field and the rings to any view, and a new step replaces the line at once.

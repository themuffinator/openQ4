Scopes · the stock layers rebuilt, round at every aspect

Each scope draws its stock layers in stock order over the view, here a 16:9 screen. Machinegun: beyond the r 182 u lens, an optical smear under a 40% gray veil; inside, a green glow rising toward the rim, a black double ring, broken posts, fine ticks, and 16 compass dots that turn with the view. Railgun: a diamond, |dx| + |dy| < 362 u, inside a black edge, with a 26% band within the edge and a gray haze beyond that thickens toward the corners. A teal glow lines the edge; #00FFFF bars flank the horizontal hair; a ring turns 40 degrees a second; one sheared #99FFFF cell shows per round; and a diamond sweeps outward every 2 s (2% in Classic, 8% in Remastered, so it reads). Nailgun: an amber glow inside four hooks and a three-band surround, with a notched ring that turns with the view. On a lock the glow brightens, the arrow lights toward the target, the lines spread and the R_Strogg readouts fade in. The aperture keeps its shape at every aspect while the veil, haze or surround reaches the screen edges. The Awakening Core Cannon's scope modification uses the machinegun scope.

Atlas - screen change

Sections 8, 9 and 13.7. The menu has three levels: home, pages and sub-pages. Home to a page is the stock change: the framing bands dock while the home layer sweeps away and the title's signal breaks up. A page is a column of the home's dark plates ending in BACK, with the focused section's summary in the content column and the prompt bar in the docked bottom band. A section that leads deeper (Campaign and Arena; Join Game, Create Server and Demos; every settings category) takes one more step: only the top band moves on, one notch pitch, and its 45-degree step slides under the page title, which lifts into the band's thin span as the parent in the path. Back always climbs one level. Click a plate or use the buttons; input during a change retargets it from the current positions.

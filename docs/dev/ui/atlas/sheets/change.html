Atlas - screen change

Sections 8 and 9. The menu has three levels: home, pages and, in Remastered, sub-pages. Home to a page is the stock change: the framing bands dock while the home layer sweeps away. A page that leads deeper (Single Player to Campaign and Arena, Multiplayer to Demos) takes one more step: only the top band moves on, one notch pitch, and its 45-degree step slides under the page title, which lifts into the band's thin span as the parent in the path. Back always climbs one level. Click a plate or use the buttons; input during a change retargets it from the current positions.

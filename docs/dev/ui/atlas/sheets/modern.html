Bound keys on the Controls page · chips in em of their strip

Each action shows up to two keys, then up to two pad buttons 0.35 em further on. The strip's font size is the chip height, so rows that grow with the text grow every chip.

Touch gameplay controls · first-party overlay

Drawn in the Marine HUD family: stick bases as thin-railed rings, buttons as cut plates around centered vector symbols, 35% at rest and 80% with the orange marker while pressed (Fire is pressed here). Fire 88 dp · jump, crouch 64 dp · pause 48 dp · hit regions at least 48 dp with 8 dp between them. Play shows no labels. The layout editor adds labels, a 24 dp snapping grid, resize handles (75–150%) and the Default, Compact and Left-handed presets (in_touchLayoutPreset 0–2). Nothing covers the crosshair.

Portrait 9:16. At the 720 × 1280 dp reference the center screen spans the width (1 u = 1.125 dp) and the thumb deck takes the 370 dp bottom band. The move stick floats in the deck's leading half; Fire, weapon, jump, crouch, zoom and reload gather in its trailing half; every other drag looks, in the deck or in the view above it. The readouts rise to the center screen's bottom edge, above the deck. Pause keeps the top leading corner, and score and chat move to the top trailing corner, clear of a camera cutout. Sizes, spacing, states and the layout editor match landscape, and each orientation keeps its own layout.

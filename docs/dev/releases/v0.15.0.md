# openQ4 0.15.0: Multiplayer Edition

**openQ4 0.15.0 is the Multiplayer Edition.** Shape Quake 4's online game your way: a Quake 2-style Classic ruleset with a grappling hook, Instagib and four more modifiers, one-word presets, buying, real spectating, and netcode that keeps your shots where you aimed. **VR players, we need you:** your own body is now in the headset, and your reports from real hardware decide what gets fixed next.

## Highlights

- 🎯 **Play multiplayer your way.** The **Classic** ruleset plays like Quake 2 deathmatch and Threewave CTF, with a grappling hook. **Instagib**, **Quad Hog**, **Vampiric Damage**, **Weapons Frenzy** and **weapon arenas** work over any game type, and presets such as `rocketarena` or `instactf` set up a whole match in one step.
- 🛒 **Buy your gear** by Quake 4's retail rules or The Awakening's, with the Dark Matter Gun and weapon mods such as bouncing hyperblaster bolts, from a new buy menu.
- 📡 **Netcode you can feel.** Your own shots draw the moment you fire, every hit you land is confirmed, a slow frame no longer sends shots where you aimed a moment before, and play is smoother above 60 fps.
- 🎥 **Spectate and cast.** Follow players by name, let the camera find the action, watch from a chase camera, and feed a live event stream to a broadcast overlay.
- 🗺️ **Servers that run themselves.** A map rotation that fits the player count, map requests, an end-of-match pick, the votes Quake players expect, team timeouts, and every server setting in Create Server's menus.
- 🥽 **Your own body in VR**, with real hands on the gun in either hand, every controller's buttons mapped, and steadier sessions. **Tell us how it runs on your headset.**
- ☀️ **Sharp shadows outdoors.** Quality and Ultra give sunlight cascaded shadow maps, so characters and nearby objects cast shadows outside. Gunfire and explosions cast shadows too.
- ⬇️ **Update from the game.** The Updates button now finds openQ4 releases, and from this release on Windows and Linux can download and install the next one for you.

## Multiplayer

### Change how a match plays
- **Classic ruleset, inspired by Quake 2.** Spawn with the blaster and 100 health. Every weapon plays its Quake 2 counterpart (the shotgun is the super shotgun, the Dark Matter Gun the BFG), armour comes in jacket, combat and body tiers, mega health drains, items respawn on Quake 2 times, and a quick second jump off stairs launches you higher. Capture the Flag scores like Threewave, and a **grappling hook** that bots use too swings you across the map. See the [Classic ruleset guide](https://github.com/themuffinator/openQ4/blob/v0.15.0/docs/user/classic-ruleset.md).
- **Modifiers for any game type.** **Instagib** (a railgun that kills in one hit, and nothing to pick up), **Quad Hog** (one Quad Damage, and only frags by or on its holder score), **Vampiric Damage** (hurting enemies heals you), **Weapons Frenzy** (fast respawns and regenerating ammo) and a **Weapon Arena** of one weapon with unlimited ammo. The server browser, loading screen and scoreboard name what is on, such as "Quad Hog Deathmatch".
- **Presets.** Pick one on Create Server's **Modifiers** tab, or type `preset rocketarena`, `railarena`, `nadefest`, `instagib`, `instactf`, `quadhog`, `vampiric`, `frenzy`, `classic` or `classicctf`; `preset standard` puts everything back. Servers can add their own.
- **Fine tuning for hosts.** Self damage, knockback and fall damage as percentages (self damage 0 makes rocket jumps free), powerups off, and weapon respawn times. See [Modifiers and presets](https://github.com/themuffinator/openQ4/blob/v0.15.0/docs/user/server-setup.md#modifiers-and-presets).
- **Buying.** The server's **Buying** option chooses Off, Quake 4's retail buy menu, or The Awakening's completed one, with its prices and rewards, the Dark Matter Gun, FC Armor Regen and four weapon mods: Bounce Shot, Firepower, Burst Fire and Chain Lightning. The new buy menu shows each item's price and why you can't buy it yet, and bought mods stay on your weapon after you die. Capture the Flag servers with buying on start their matches again. See [Buying in multiplayer](https://github.com/themuffinator/openQ4/blob/v0.15.0/docs/user/buying.md).

### Online play
- **Your shots draw the moment you fire.** Machinegun, shotgun and railgun tracers and wall marks no longer trail a round trip behind your aim. The server still decides every hit on a player.
- **Every hit is confirmed.** The hit sound, crosshair flash and hit marker no longer go missing when one network update is lost.
- **A slow frame no longer costs you a shot.** After a frame that took too long, your aim and fire still reach the server in time, and the first hit of a match no longer hitches.
- **Smoother above 60 fps**, with updates that arrive on an even beat, projectiles drawn on their true path, and no second hitch after the first.
- **High ping is no longer silent:** from about 250 ms, broadcast sounds used to be thrown away. A lost update no longer hides a kill or a hit either.
- **Harder to break.** Forged packets can no longer knock you off a server, modified clients can no longer flood or stall it, and joins work through NAT64, small-MTU VPNs and networks that drop split packets.
- **Your weapon comes up on time** after a respawn on a jittery connection.

### Hosting
- **Create Server shows everything.** Every setting sits on four tabs (**Match**, **Rules**, **Modifiers** and **Server**), with sliders for limits and player counts, and the options you could once only type: map rotation, map requests, item timers, timeouts per team, spectator lock and an idle timeout.
- **A map rotation that fits the server.** The server finds every installed map by itself and skips maps that are too big or too small for the players present. Players queue maps with `mymap q4dm3`, and with `g_mapPick 1` everyone picks the next map from three. Operators can tune each map in an optional `mapdb.txt`.
- **Votes the way Quake players type them.** `callvote` (or `cv`) with `nextmap`, `shuffle`, `balance`, `scorelimit`, `roundlimit`, `ruleset`, `instagib`, `overtime`, `cointoss`, `random` and `readyall`, plus operator rules for vote percentage and limits. Most of them are on the in-match Vote page as well.
- **Team timeouts and fairer servers.** `timeout` and `timein` on casual servers, a pause when a player disconnects, idle players moved to spectators, and limits that stay as you set them across map changes.
- **No hitch when someone joins.** Every player model loads with the map.

### Playing
- **Smart weapon switching.** A pickup only takes over from the gauntlet, the starting machinegun or an empty weapon, so running over a shotgun keeps your railgun up. A new **Last Weapon** key (G) and a **weapon order** you rank in Player Setup decide the rest.
- **Chat tokens.** Write `#h`, `#a`, `#w`, `#l` or `#n` in a chat line and it becomes your health, armour, weapon, location or nearby team mates.
- **Hit tones, item timers and team locations.** Hits can sound higher or lower by damage, servers can show powerup and armour timers, the Competitive HUD names where each team mate is, and the crosshair can show your ammo.
- **Player Setup gains a HUD tab**, and the in-match menu now reaches Audio and Display settings.
- **Your controller rumbles** when you fire and when you are hit, in multiplayer and the campaigns.

### Spectating, casting and demos
- **Follow anyone.** `follow <name>` takes part of a name, `follow auto killer|action|leader|powerup|flag` lets the camera find the action, and `followview first|third|chase` picks the view. Casual spectators can now follow both teams.
- **Cast a match.** With `g_casterAccess 1`, a spectator can see both teams' health and armour without a managed match, and `g_matchEventStream 1` writes scores, kills and flags as they happen for a broadcast overlay.
- **Recordings less than half the size** that seek in about a second, survive a crash, and play on past a damaged section. `compressDemo` shrinks an older recording.
- **Multi-view demos play again** from the menu. 0.14.1 stopped them at the map load.

See the new [multiplayer guide](https://github.com/themuffinator/openQ4/blob/v0.15.0/docs/user/multiplayer.md), the one place to start for joining, hosting, game types, rules, spectating and voting.

## VR: we need testers

openQ4 plays Quake 4 in VR through OpenXR, on SteamVR, Meta Quest Link or Monado, with OpenGL or Vulkan. Everything in it has been built and checked against a simulated headset, **but no one has yet confirmed it on a real one.** If you own a PC VR headset, please try 0.15.0 and tell us how it runs, good or bad. The [VR guide](https://github.com/themuffinator/openQ4/blob/v0.15.0/docs/user/vr.md#quick-start) gets you in within five steps, and its [Reporting a problem](https://github.com/themuffinator/openQ4/blob/v0.15.0/docs/user/vr.md#reporting-a-problem) section lists what to send.

New in 0.15.0:
- **Your own body.** Look down and you have a chest, legs and feet where you stand, and arms that reach the gun. Your free hand follows its controller and returns to the gun for two-handed grips and reloads. Choose it under **Settings → Game Options → Virtual Reality → Your Body**.
- **Real hands on the gun, in either hand.** Left-handed players now see each gun held by its own mirrored hands, and the gun still reads the right way round.
- **Every controller, every action.** Valve Index, HTC Vive wands and Windows Mixed Reality controllers now reach jump, crouch, reload and the flashlight, and holding an Index controller no longer switches weapons by accident.
- **Steadier sessions.** A brief stall in the headset's software no longer drops you to the desktop, and a missing optional feature such as vibration no longer stops VR.
- **Comfort and facing.** Hold the weapon-hand stick to recentre; levels, saves and teleporters face you where the game intends; the vignette centres properly on angled displays; and the gun no longer shoots through a wall you hold it against.
- **Refresh rate and clocks** (`vr_refreshRate`, `vr_performanceLevel`) for headsets that let a game choose them, such as Quest over Link.

## Graphics and performance

- **Cascaded sunlight shadows on Quality and Ultra.** Your own shadow, other characters' and nearby objects' shadows now show up outdoors, where one stretched shadow map used to lose most of them. Shadows also sit closer to what casts them and no longer stop at the edge of the cascades.
- **Gunfire and explosions cast shadows** on Quality, Ultra and Maximum, and more explosions light up the room.
- **Character self-shadows** (optional, Display → Image Quality): an arm shades the chest behind it.
- **Ambient occlusion you can choose.** Display → Effects now offers Off, SSAO, HBAO and GTAO, and SSAO is smooth instead of dithered.
- **Smooth startup and loading screens.** Both animate at your display's refresh rate the whole way through instead of about 25 frames a second with pauses, without loading any slower, and a level starts the moment its loading screen ends.
- **Texture settings apply without freezing the game**, reloading only what a change affects, spread over a few frames.
- **Lighter menus and effects.** Menus respond in a tenth of a millisecond, The Awakening's snowy menus stopped costing a whole frame, the animated crosshair costs a fraction of what it did, and busy battles spend less time on particles, collision and decals.
- **More texture pack formats:** PNG, BC4/BC5, BC6H HDR and DX10 DDS, with packs larger than video memory scaled down on Vulkan instead of resetting the driver.
- **Experimental:** shadowed volumetric light shafts, and parallax occlusion for mods' PBR height maps.

## Campaigns, interface and sound

- **Switch between Quake 4 and The Awakening in place**, in two to four seconds instead of a full engine restart.
- **The Awakening keeps getting better:** its own frozen pause and settings menus, a loading screen for every level, a Falcon gunship fight that can finally be won, a freeze gun that recharges, restored dialogue and music, and repaired squad scenes from the opening dropship to Cryofac.
- **Settings that are easier to find**, with new Accessibility and Effects tabs, and Frame Rate Cap, Anisotropic Filtering, Shadow Maps and Motion Blur now in the menu. Mouse and controller settings moved to Controls.
- **View Kick and Screen Shake settings** let you steady the camera when you fire.
- **Volume as Quake 4 set it.** Master volume goes up to 200% again, as in the original game, and the ambient drones in levels such as Hub 1 and Dispersal play as level sounds at their original loudness instead of following the Music slider.
- **Subtitles for radio chatter**, speaker names that work, and a marker for speech with no transcript.
- **PNG screenshots**, and screenshots from the pause menu.
- **Russian gameplay text**, beside the menus.
- **Optional Enhanced Physics** for ragdolls, crates and debris (off by default while it is tested).
- **Mods play over The Awakening**, and a mod's old copy of openQ4's text no longer blanks the menus.

## Fixes worth knowing

- Switching renderers no longer turns the screen white while the game reloads.
- Servers whose Quake 4 paks are installed twice let players in again.
- Bodies placed in levels no longer blow apart on Linux, which now does its math exactly as Windows does.
- Borderless and window changes on Wayland no longer stop the game from starting.
- The classic menus' cursor reaches the edges of a widescreen menu.

## Upgrade Notes

- **Install the complete 0.15.0 package** into its own folder, or replace your previous openQ4 install entirely. Don't mix files from different versions. Your Quake 4 installation is still required, and your settings, bindings and saves carry over.
- **Multiplayer needs 0.15.0 on both sides.** 0.15.0 speaks network protocol 2.42 and 0.14.1 speaks 2.41, so they refuse each other and leave each other out of the server list. Retail Quake 4 and mods built for the original game DLLs still can't join.
- **Demos:** recordings made by 0.15.0 don't play in 0.14.1 or earlier. Older recordings still play in 0.15.0.
- **Server operators:** without an `si_mapCycle` list, the rotation now plays every installed map that suits the game type. Set `g_mapRotation 3` to keep Quake 4's map cycle script.
- **Auto Weapon Switch** moves from Always to the new Smart once. The new Last Weapon key and the hook on Mouse4 reach new profiles and a reset of the controls; existing profiles keep their bindings.
- **Volume:** if you had turned master volume up past 100%, openQ4 saved it as 100%; raise it again under **Settings → Audio**. The console's `s_volume_dB` never worked in openQ4 and is gone; use `s_volume`.
- **Graphics presets changed again.** If you use Quality or Ultra, the graphics check offers the updated preset once at startup.
- **Mod authors:** the game module API is now 61; game modules built for earlier versions don't load.
- **macOS:** our preview macOS packages target Apple Silicon/arm64. Intel Mac packages are not published, and Rosetta is not supported. They use a Metal bridge around the OpenGL renderer, not a native Metal renderer; `platform_backend=native` remains comparison-only diagnostic infrastructure, not a release backend. Qualification is tracked in [docs/dev/macos-signoff-evidence.md](https://github.com/themuffinator/openQ4/blob/v0.15.0/docs/dev/macos-signoff-evidence.md). Signed and notarized builds for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs are offered separately by [Mac Source Ports](https://www.macsourceports.com/game/quake4).
- **Updates:** this release installs the usual way. From 0.15.0 on, the Updates button can install later releases from the game on Windows (zip or the installer's default folder) and Linux (tar.xz); `com_updateCheck 0` turns off the startup check.

## Known Limitations

- **VR** has not been confirmed on real headset hardware. Please [report how it runs](https://github.com/themuffinator/openQ4/blob/v0.15.0/docs/user/vr.md#reporting-a-problem).
- **A standalone Meta Quest build** can be built from source but is not published, and no headset has run it yet.
- **The new multiplayer modes** have been tested with bots and automated clients; please report how they play with people.
- **Enhanced Physics** stays off by default while it is tested, and **The Awakening** is still built from unfinished alpha content.
- **Vulkan** is supported on Windows and experimental on Linux and macOS. Windows ARM64 is experimental; Linux ARM64 and macOS are previews.
